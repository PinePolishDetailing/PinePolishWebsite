About Pine Polish

My name is Talon Lewis, and I am the 13-year-old founder of Pine Polish Cart Detailing. What started as a simple idea to earn some extra money quickly grew into something more meaningful, a commitment to serving my community and helping golf cart owners take pride in their rides.

I created Pine Polish with a clear goal: to provide careful, high-quality golf cart detailing using reliable equipment and proven products. Even as a young entrepreneur, I understand the importance of responsibility, trust, and attention to detail. Every cart will be treated with care and respect, as if it were my own.

Although Pine Polish is just getting started, my focus is on building a reputation for quality work, strong work ethic, and dependable service. Customers can trust that when I take on a job, I will give it my full effort and attention.

This business is more than a side hustle—it is an opportunity to grow, serve others, and deliver results that make a difference in our community.
